# asset credits

everything here is self-hosted. nothing is hotlinked.

## generated

`banner.gif`, `construction.gif`, `rule.gif` and `marquee.svg` are drawn by
`tools/gen.py` from the bitmap font in `tools/font.py`.
`uv run tools/gen.py` rebuilds them.
